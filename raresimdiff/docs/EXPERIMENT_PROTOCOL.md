# Research evaluation protocol

## A. Generator quality: learned vs procedural (`scripts/evaluate_model.sh`)
At matched (event, severity) conditions (default 4 events × 4 severities × 200 scenarios) both generators are validated in MuJoCo and scored on:
1. physical validity (valid ratio, reject reasons, simulation-instability rate, repair magnitude)
2. diversity (mean pairwise L1 in bound-normalised space; occupied cells of a 4-feature grid)
3. conditioning accuracy (event actually exhibited; |recomputed severity − requested|)
4. rare-event coverage (hits on grid cells that are sparse (≤2 samples) in the training set)
5. novelty (nearest-neighbour distance to the train set; memorisation rate = NN distance < 0.01)
6. difficulty (failure rate of the fixed scripted baseline on accepted scenarios)

Diverse or novel scenarios are **not** automatically useful for learning. Reading rule: the learned generator "adds coverage" only if, at equal accepted
counts, it increases rare-cell coverage or high-severity mass **without** a larger reject rate or memorisation. Otherwise report "no added value".

## B. Downstream robot-learning study (to be run after the official training; NOT executed in the MVP)
Fix: simulator, robot, task set, evaluation scenarios, policy architecture, optimiser, total number of gradient steps, seeds (≥5).

| Arm | Training data |
|---|---|
| 1 Baseline | N_base nominal + procedurally generated base scenarios |
| 2 +Procedural | arm 1 + M procedurally generated scenarios (matched severity histogram) |
| 3 +Diffusion | arm 1 + M scenarios from the frozen generator (validated + accepted) |

Same M and same rollout budget in arms 2 and 3. Evaluation uses held-out `test` and `ood_test` parents plus a **fixed real-parameter-free** challenge set
(never generated by either generator). Metrics (`policy_integration/metrics.py`): task completion, failure by event family, contact/collision
violations, recovery success, nominal-task regression, performance by severity bin. Report mean ± bootstrap CI over seeds; test arm 3 vs arm 2.
Policy learners: BC/ACT and Diffusion Policy need genuine expert demonstrations (none exist yet); PPO/SAC can use `ScenarioEnv` directly.

## C. Claims allowed
Only simulation results. No sim-to-real claim without real-robot evidence. CPU smoke tests and random-weight pipelines are structural evidence only.
